## What this proves already
- The query runs on the real QA data and returns 14 Burger King plazas.
- **COGS = Begin + Purchase - End - Transfer out, exactly, on every plaza** (last column is 0.00 everywhere). This
  explains why the old PDF did not add up at 5 plazas: transfers out are part of COGS but not shown.
- Variance = COGS - Theocost - Waste on every row.
