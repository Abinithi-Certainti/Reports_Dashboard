UI options: one choice per area

Three areas, each with A, B and C. Reply like "1B, 2B, 3A". Area 1 and 2 would change every report; area 3 is the new look for Sales and Margin with Budget.

AREA 1 Number cards (top of every report)

1AGlass cards with a trend lineToday's look: big number, small line of the last days.
1BProgress ringsEach number with a ring showing how far it is toward budget or the previous period, plus the change.
1COne slim stripAll numbers in one thin bar with dividers. Takes the least space, leaves more room for the tables.

AREA 2 Tables (every report)

2AClean rowsToday's look: plain rows, thin lines.
2BRows with in-cell bars and coloured varianceEach amount has a soft bar behind it so the biggest stand out; variance is green when good, red when bad.
2CRow cardsEach row becomes a small card with a green or red edge. Easy to scan and works well on phones; less good for many columns.
