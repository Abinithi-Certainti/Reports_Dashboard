# Market Category Report - QA probe results, 2026-09-25 (tools/market_probe.sql + INFO.REFRESHPOLICIES)

## 13. PLU -> product mapping
No table in any schema maps a PLU to a product. Tables with plu columns are the POS order / void tables (and their
staging copies); product_num appears only in the weekly COGS tables. `invoice_detail.vendor_product_number` is a
supplier number, not a PLU. **The Net-Chef PLU mapping has no home in the new database.**

Also seen: a `public` schema with its own pos_order_details / pos_order_voids, and tables `weekly_cogs_bk`,
`weekly_cogs_prod_num_bk`.

## 14. Market sales in master.pos_order_details (QA)
One day only: 2026-05-19, 18,548 order lines.

## INFO.REFRESHPOLICIES (Market Sale-2)
- Same SQL as Market Sale, with `Endday >= @RangeStart AND Endday < @RangeEnd`.
- Rolling window: granularity 3 (year), 2 periods -> keeps about the last 2 years; refreshes the last 7 days
  (incremental granularity 0 = day, 7 periods).
- This explains the gap: Market Sale holds 2021-01 to 2022-04 (loaded once); Market Sale-2 drops anything older
  than its rolling window, so May 2022 - Dec 2023 is gone from the model.
