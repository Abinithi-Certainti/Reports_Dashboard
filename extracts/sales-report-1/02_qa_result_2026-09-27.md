pasted), so which login ran them is not recorded. Real figures are kept outside git (demo/private-data/).

## What QA holds
- POS: **one store, 101518**, 2026-09-06 to 2026-09-21. The first and last day are part days (10 and 5 orders).
  Its plaza and brand are not known: QA has no netsuite_location_mapping.
- 65 of its 18,649 orders are Cash Drop and are left out, as the report does.
- Card fees and beer deposits are 0 on every day.
- employee_pay_summary: pay_date is text written as YYYY-MM-DD, so `pay_date::date` works. Locations are
  "PLAZA BRAND" (for example "CAMBRIDGE NORTH TIM HORTONS"), the same shape as the old report's LB name, for many
  plazas, not only store 101518.
- Pay categories on QA include ShiftPrem, Unpaid, Vacation and Hol1.0. The report counts only Reg, OT1.5 and Hol1.5,
  so those rows are left out. ShiftPrem rows repeat the hours of Reg rows, so counting them would double the hours.
